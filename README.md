## 📌 Project Overview

This project covers two interconnected manufacturing tasks, combining hands-on workshop skills with modern CAD/CAM simulation tools. It targets real-world engineering competencies: mechanical design, precision machining, hydraulic actuation, and digital manufacturing workflows.

---

## 🦾 Part 1 — Hydraulic Robotic Arm

> Designed and fabricated a **syringe-driven hydraulic robotic arm** capable of lifting and transferring marshmallows into a cup of water.

```
Base Platform  →  Arm Segments  →  Gripper Mechanism
     ↕                  ↕                  ↕
Hydraulic Syringe  ──  Tubing  ──  Actuation Syringe
```

**Key Highlights**
- Modelled in **CATIA V5** — from 2D sketch to full 3D assembly
- Components laser-cut from **4 mm cardboard**
- Assembled using bolts, nails, zip ties, and hot glue
- Hydraulic actuation via water-filled syringe tubes
- Arm reach constrained to **200–300 mm**, base ≤ **250 mm**

---

## ⚙️ Part 2 — CNC Axle Machining

> Machined a **precision steel axle** designed to support the front wheels of a radio-controlled car — simulated in Inventor CAM and manufactured in the workshop.

### Routing Sheet

| Step | Operation | Machine |
|------|-----------|---------|
| 1 | Raw material cut to length | Saw |
| 2 | Facing — flat reference surface | Lathe |
| 3 | Turning — diameter reduction | Lathe |
| 4 | Grooving — retaining ring seats | Lathe |
| 5 | Drilling — central & side holes | Drill Press |
| 6 | Milling — flat end surfaces | Mill |
| 7 | Threading — both ends | Lathe |

### Final Measured Dimensions

| Feature | Measured |
|---------|----------|
| Body length | 130.5 mm |
| Body diameter | 18.65 mm |
| External thread diameter | 9.65 mm |
| Central hole diameter | 8.5 mm |
| Side hole diameter | 6.25 mm |
| Central hole offset from end | 65 mm |
| Side holes offset from end | 14.4 mm |
| Milling depth | 0.34 mm |

### CAM Simulation (Inventor CAM)
- 18 operations across both setups
- 9 tools — turning, drilling, milling, threading, grooving
- Estimated cycle time: **18 min 49 s**
- Workpiece stock: 23 × 23 × 161 mm → finished part: 19 × 19 × 130 mm
